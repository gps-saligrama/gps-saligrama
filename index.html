HONORING OUR ROOTS

Our Story & Mercantile Heritage

Serving the households of Saligrama, Kota, Sasthan, and surrounding coastal hamlets with authentic provisions, pure cooking ingredients, and personalized care.

At GOPAL PUTTU SHANBHOGUE & SONS, grocery shopping has always been a warm neighborhood tradition. Prominently positioned directly opposite the historic Shree Anjaneya Temple on Saligrama Main Road, our shop is the early morning stop for devotees, home chefs, and village elders.

From premium grains like Sona Masoori and red boiled rice (Kuchalakki) to fragrant Byadgi chillies, cold-pressed coconut oil, and sacred camphor, our shelves reflect the authentic tastes and traditions of Karnataka. We stand firmly for honest weighing, unadulterated provisions, and fair pricing without compromise.
